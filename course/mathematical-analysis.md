## MIT OpenCourseWare (English, PDF)

### 1. MIT 18.100B — Real Analysis (Spring 2025)
**Prof. Tobias Holck Colding** | Level: Undergraduate/Graduate

Complete Real Analysis lecture notes with 23 lecture notes + 2 reviews, all in PDF.

**Link:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-100b-real-analysis-spring-2025/pages/lecture-notes/

**Contents:**
- Introduction to real numbers
- Sequences and convergence
- Monotone Convergence Theorem
- Cauchy Convergence Theorem
- Bolzano-Weierstrass Theorem
- Series and convergence tests
- Limsup and Liminf
- Continuous functions
- Extreme Value Theorem, Intermediate Value Theorem
- Metric spaces
- Open and closed sets, compactness
- Derivatives, Rolle, Mean Value Theorem, L'Hopital, Taylor
- Riemann integral
- Fundamental Theorem of Calculus
- Pointwise and uniform convergence
- Power series
- Ordinary differential equations (ODE)
- Picard-Lindelof Theorem

**Complete single file:** Available as a single PDF with all lecture notes.
